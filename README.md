# Matt's LEARN Tools

Browser-based tools for creating visual assets and interactive content for LEARN (Blackboard Ultra), and for working with course exports.

**Live site:** https://mattoteacher.github.io/learn-tools/

## About and re-use

- These tools are not an official University of Edinburgh service, and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthology Inc. (Blackboard).
- The tools run entirely in your browser. Information you enter stays on your own device.
- You're welcome to review, learn from or adapt the **code** for your own needs.
- That permission does **not** cover the University of Edinburgh name, logos and crest, the icons (used with permission and attribution), or image templates adapted from Edinburgh Medical School Digital Education Unit work. Please remove or replace these if you adapt the tools.
- The tools are provided as-is, without warranty. Always check results before relying on them.

Full details, including privacy and contact information: [About these tools](https://mattoteacher.github.io/learn-tools/about.html)
