Course export converter

Turn a Learn Ultra course export into Word documents and files a student can download and keep. Everything happens in your browser, so the course files are never uploaded anywhere.

  1. Choose the export file

    How do I export a course from Learn?

    In your Ultra course, open the More options menu (⋯) at the top of Course Content and choose Export course. Learn prepares the package in the background. When it's ready, download the zip file it creates.

    Use the export exactly as you downloaded it. Don't unzip it first.
